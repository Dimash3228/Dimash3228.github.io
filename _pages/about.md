I am a data analyst and AI product developer at the **National Medical Research Center for Hematology, Ministry of Health of Russia** (Moscow). My work focuses on developing machine learning models and AI-driven tools for clinical data analysis in hematology and oncology.

My research interests lie at the intersection of **artificial intelligence, computational biology, and biomedicine**.
