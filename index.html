Selected Research & Development

My current work is centered on two areas: machine learning on real clinical data and AI systems for working with clinical information.

Research

Machine Learning for Differential Diagnosis in Hematology

My master’s research examined machine-learning methods for the differential diagnosis of hematological diseases using real clinical and laboratory data. I worked on data preparation, model development, validation, and analysis of diagnostic predictions. The project formed the basis of my first-author publication in Transfusiology.

Development

RAG Prototype for Clinical Information

I am developing a RAG-based prototype for working with clinical information. The project explores retrieval from medical documents and structured patient data, interaction with clinical information systems, and generation of grounded summaries. The prototype remains under active development.
